# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute without being
harassed, insulted, or dismissed. That applies regardless of experience level,
language, nationality, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
appearance, or technical opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good faith. Critique the code, not the person.
- Be concrete. "This breaks when the session has no user messages" helps; "this is
  bad" does not.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change, and explain why.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through the **Security** tab's private reporting channel, or
contact a maintainer directly if you already have a way to reach one. Reports are
handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may warn,
temporarily block, or permanently block a participant.

This document is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
